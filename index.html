📞 General Emergency

While waiting for help:

  • Stay calm and check the scene for safety
  • Do not move the person unless necessary
  • Keep the person warm and comfortable
  • Gather any medications the person takes
  • Be ready to describe what happened and your location
  • If in rural area, send someone to flag down help on the road
